Privacy should be simple.

Blur Stuff is a privacy-first media anonymization tool designed to make removing sensitive information from your files quick and easy.

What is Blur Stuff?

Blur Stuff automatically detects sensitive information in your images, videos, and documents and lets you anonymize it before sharing.

You can detect and anonymize things such as faces, license plates, text, and personal information without manually editing every part of your file.

Why we built it

Sharing photos, videos, and documents often means accidentally sharing information that was never meant to be public.

Blur Stuff aims to make protecting that information accessible to everyone — without requiring complicated editing software or a long manual process.

Built with privacy in mind

No account required

Use the core anonymization workflow without creating an account.

Minimal data

Blur Stuff is designed to process your files rather than build a permanent collection of your media.

Open source friendly

The project is built with transparency and inspectable software in mind.

What can it anonymize?

Faces

Automatically detect and anonymize faces.

License Plates

Hide vehicle license plates before sharing.

Text

Detect and anonymize text found in your files.

Personal Information

Help remove potentially identifying information.

Open source

Blur Stuff is developed as an open-source-friendly project. You can explore the code, see how the application works, and follow its development on GitHub.
